### User is in the Philippines (2026-11-25)

- **YouTube:** Shorts reach viewers by interest and language more than by the uploader's
  country, so English gaming Shorts posted at US prime time can reach US viewers. SEGA stays
  on YouTube first.
- **TikTok:** a new account's posts go mostly to its own country, decided by the account's
  setup (SIM, network, region), not the posting time. Clipping Nation's guide "How to Target a
  Tier 1 Audience" (https://app.notion.com/p/41a582c82d888176b58cc0f1bc4b736e) tells clippers
  outside Tier 1 to create the account on a paid VPN set to the US, keep everything in
  English, stay on one server, and scroll/engage 10–15 min before each post. That is the
  user's call: TikTok can still read the SIM's country, and VPN use can get an account limited.
