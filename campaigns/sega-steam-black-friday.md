- [ ] **Other:** captions must be genuine and in your own words (no copying other clippers).

## Notes
- Best fit for a 0-subscriber channel: no follower minimum, highest pay per view, paid from
  ~1K views, gaming content with big franchises (Sonic, Persona, Like a Dragon, Metaphor).
- Pipeline fit: the source is one short official sale video, not a long talk, so clips are
  per-game cuts with on-screen text, not transcript-picked moments. Render with the original
  audio only; no added music.
- Keep the 3–5 clips meaningfully different (different featured game per clip) — the channel
  rule against near-duplicates and SEGA's "same video more than 5 times" rule both apply.
- TikTok: caption tag is also @sega_west. Turn on TikTok's Branded content toggle (#Ad post).
- **Cross-posting unclear:** "Spam posting: the same video on multiple accounts" is banned,
  but "Posting the same video more than 5 times" suggests some reposting is tolerated. Ask
  Clipping Culture in the campaign chat before putting one clip on both YouTube and TikTok;
  until then, give each platform different clips.
- User is in the Philippines: post on YouTube in English at US prime time (11 AM–2 PM or
  5–9 PM ET = 12–3 AM or 6–10 AM PH time) and check each Short's audience-by-country in
  YouTube Studio before relying on it. Views from PH count as "other countries" ($5/1K), but
  the post or channel must still be 50%+ US/Canada.
